      <h1>Angela's Cinnammon Roll Recipe</h1>
   </ul>
    <br>
      <h2>ingredients</h2>
      <h3>For the dough:</h3>
   <ul>
      <li>1 egg plus 1 egg yolk</li>
      <li>3/4 cup warm milk</li>
      <li>1/4 cup white sugar</li>
      <li>2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast</li>
      <li>3 cups bread flour</li>
      <li>1/2 teaspoon salt</li>
   </ul>
     <br>
      <h4>For the filling</h4>
   <ul>
      <li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
      <li>1/4 cup butter</li>
      <li>2/3 cup dark brown sugar</li>
      <li>1/2 cup brown sugar</li>
   </ul>
     <br>
     <h5>instructions</h5>
     <ol>
      <li>Mix the warm milk, yeast, and sugar in a bowl.</li>
      <li>Add the egg and melted butter, then mix well.</li>
      <li>Slowly add the flour and salt until a soft dough forms.</li>
      <li>Knead the dough for about 8–10 minutes.</li>
      <li>Cover the dough and let it rise for about 1 hour.</li>
      <li>Roll the dough into a large rectangle.</li>
      <li>Spread the softened butter over the dough.</li>
      <li>Sprinkle the brown sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla evenly.</li>
      <li>Roll the dough tightly and cut it into pieces.</li>
      <li>Place the rolls in a baking pan and let them rest for 20 minutes.</li>
     </ol>
